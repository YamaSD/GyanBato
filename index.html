FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answer for frequently asked questions

Is GyanBato free to use?

Yes! You can create a free account and access a generous set of practice questions, mock tests, and analytics. Premium plans unlock additional features like unlimited mock tests and detailed analytics.

Which exams does GyanBato support?

GyanBato supports a growing range of competitive and professional exams. Choose your exam from your dashboard to get practice questions tailored to your preparation needs.

How does the level-based system work?

Questions are organized into different difficulty levels. Your performance helps you identify your current level and focus your preparation on areas where you need the most improvement.

How are practice questions selected?

Practice questions are selected based on your chosen exam, subject, difficulty level, and previous performance to create a focused learning experience.

Can I track my progress?

Yes. Your dashboard provides insights into your accuracy, completed questions, mock-test results, and overall preparation progress.

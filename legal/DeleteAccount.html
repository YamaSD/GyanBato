Account Deletion & Data Removal

Learn how to delete your GyanBato account and request the removal of all associated personal data in compliance with Google Play Store policies.

Steps to Request Deletion
  • 1
    Submit Deletion Request
    Fill out the request form below with your registered email and details. You can request deletion even if you have already uninstalled the application.
  • 2
    Verification & Confirmation
    Our support team will verify your registered user profile and email ownership to prevent unauthorized deletion.
  • 3
    Permanent Data Erasure
    Once verified, all your account records and personal data will be permanently deleted from our active servers within 7-14 business days.
What Data Will Be Permanently Removed?

Upon processing your request, the following information is permanently erased and cannot be recovered:

Account Profile (Name, Email, Role)
Registered Exam Entries
Practice Test & Quiz Histories
Submitted Answers & Submissions
Earned Marks & Published Results
Device tokens & Refresh keys
